Get to know us →

Fit Squad Security is a UK-based professional security company built around the owner’s experience in a wide range of security roles, from unarmed guarding to event security and CCTV operations. The business started with a goal to provide affordable, reliable security while treating both clients and staff with respect, and has grown into a dedicated team of qualified security professionals.

Our mission is to deliver high-quality, dependable security services tailored to the needs of each client, ensuring safety for people, property and events with professionalism, integrity, and a responsive approach. We strive to make top-tier security accessible without overcharging clients or undervaluing our guards.

We envision becoming a trusted security partner across industries, known for dependable protection, cost-effective solutions, and a team that consistently goes beyond expectations to safeguard what matters to our clients.

We are guided by respect for both clients and employees, a commitment to ethical and transparent service, and a focus on professionalism and accountability in everything we do. Our values ensure that every engagement prioritises safety, integrity, and quality
